Quick overview

What the app does

The app is built for counter service: the merchant shows a QR code, the customer creates their pass, then the merchant scans that customer on each visit to add stamps or unlock a reward.

1

Sign up

The customer scans the merchant public QR code and creates their personal loyalty card.

2

Scan

The merchant scans the customer private QR code at the counter to add a stamp or credit a purchase.

3

Re-engage

Customers with an active Wallet pass can receive offers directly through Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

Step 01

Log in to the dashboard

The merchant lands on the login screen. They enter their email and password, then tap Sign in. Once logged in, the app loads the merchant data.

Step 02

Wait for loading

The black screen with the TO BE SEEN logo is the startup animation. It means the dashboard is loading. The merchant can wait or tap the screen if the animation invites them to enter.

LoadingStartup
Step 03

Home and public QR

The Home screen shows the merchant public QR code. This QR is used to onboard a new customer into the loyalty program. The Open customer QR button opens it full-screen for easy display.

  • Scan opens the counter scanner.
  • Clients shows the customer database.
  • Offers is used to send a Wallet offer.
  • Rewards is used to manage rewards.

Step 04

View customers

The Clients tab groups registered customers. The Wallet passes number corresponds to customers who can receive Wallet updates or offers.

  • The Wallet badge means the customer is reachable.
  • The number on the right shows their points or stamps.
  • The code under the name is the customer identifier.
CRMWalletPoints
Step 05

Open the Scanner

The Scanner screen is used at the counter. The logic is always the same: scan the customer QR, confirm the customer, then add a stamp or give a reward.

  • Open camera activates the camera.
  • The manual field lets you paste a code or URL if the camera does not work.
  • The action button stays disabled until a customer is detected.
Counter modeOpen camera
Step 06

Show the public QR full-screen

From Home, the full-screen view shows the merchant public QR. This is what a new customer scans to create their loyalty card.

Copy link copies the pass link so it can be sent manually if needed.

Step 07

Scan the customer private QR

When the camera is open, the merchant points it at the customer QR. As soon as the QR is recognized, the app automatically fills the field with the detected URL or code.

In stamp mode, the main button becomes Add stamp.

Step 08

Customer found

The Customer scanned message confirms that the QR was recognized. The merchant can then add the stamp, credit the purchase or continue to a reward.

Step 09

Check the visit

After validation, the app shows the latest visit in Latest visit. The daily scan counter increases and the customer receives their stamp or points.

If the action was just completed, the app briefly locks the button to avoid an accidental double add.

Step 10

Give a reward

When a reward is available, the Rewards available after this stamp block appears. The Redeem button validates the reward at the counter.

Example: if the reward costs 1 stamp and the customer has enough, the merchant taps Redeem and gives the reward to the customer.

Reward readyRedeem
Rewards, profile and setup

Advanced screens

These screens are used to manage program rules, customize the card, send offers and explain how the end customer creates their pass.

Step 11

Create a reward

The Rewards screen defines what the customer earns and how many points or stamps are required.

  • Reward name: reward name.
  • Minimum: threshold required to earn it.
  • Create: creates the reward.
  • Active / Draft: enables or disables the reward in the Scanner.
RewardsMinimumActive
Step 12

Understand the Wait state

When a reward is being validated, the button may show Wait. This prevents multiple taps and avoids charging the customer twice.

Wait for the action to finish before scanning again or giving another reward.

Customize the design

Step 16

Understand the Wallet card

The Wallet card shows the business name, stamp count, member, latest offer and customer QR. This is the card the customer shows at the counter.

When the merchant scans this QR, the customer is found in the app.

Apple WalletCustomer QRLatest offer
Step 17

Edit loyalty rules

In Manage, the Rules section controls the program.

  • Stamp reward: final reward.
  • Stamp goal: number of stamps required.
  • Expiration: validity period.
  • QR link: public link used by the merchant QR.

After editing, tap Save, then refresh Wallet cards from Profile.

RulesStamp goalSave
Step 18

Create a Wallet offer

In Offers, the merchant writes a title and message. The Review send to X button prepares the send to customers with an active Wallet card.

  • Save keeps the offer as a draft.
  • Wallet is the current sending channel.
  • Review send to 50 means 50 Wallet cards can receive the offer.
OffersWallet pushReview
Step 19

Confirm the send

Before sending, the app shows a confirmation. The merchant reviews the title, message and audience.

  • Cancel cancels.
  • Send now sends the offer immediately.

Tip: do not send too often. One to three offers per week is enough in most cases.

Confirm sendSend now
Step 20

Create the customer pass

When a customer scans the merchant public QR, they land on this page. They must enter their name, email and optionally their phone number.

Once registered, their private QR is generated and they can add the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

Client signupPrivate QRWallet
Step 21

Read the created customer pass

After signup, the customer sees their personal pass. The top confirms that the pass is ready, then the card shows their stamp count, name, member ID and reward.

This QR is the customer private QR. It is the one the merchant scans at the counter, not the merchant public QR.

Pass readyCustomer QRStamps
Step 22

Add the pass to Wallet

At the bottom of the customer pass, two buttons let the customer add the card to their phone.

  • Add to Apple Wallet installs the card on iPhone.
  • Add to Google Wallet installs the card on Android.
  • The Stamps, Offers and Private QR shortcuts summarize what the card contains.

Once the card is added, the customer can open it from Wallet on every store visit.

Apple WalletGoogle WalletPrivate QR
Offers

Send a Wallet offer

In the Offers tab, the merchant writes a title and message, then confirms the send. The offer is sent only to customers with an active Wallet card.

  • Save keeps the offer as a draft.
  • Review send to X prepares the send.
  • Send now sends the offer after confirmation.
Profile

Update cards

In Profile, the Refresh all Wallet cards button pushes the latest changes to installed Wallet cards.

  • Use it after changing the logo or color.
  • Use it after changing the number of stamps.
  • Use it if a Wallet card looks outdated.
Install the app

Use Loyalty Pass like a real phone app.

Merchants do not need to download anything from the App Store or Play Store. They can add the web app to the Home Screen and open it like a normal app.

Use this section during onboarding after the merchant has their login details.

iPhone / SafariAndroid / Chrome
For iPhone / Safari

Add Loyalty Pass to the iPhone Home Screen

  1. Open tbsloyaltypass.com/login in Safari.
  2. Tap the Safari Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Rename the app to Loyalty Pass if needed.
  5. Keep Open as Web App enabled, then tap Add.
  6. The merchant can now open Loyalty Pass directly from the iPhone Home Screen.
For Android / Chrome

Add Loyalty Pass to the Android Home screen

  1. Open tbsloyaltypass.com/login in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app. The wording depends on the Android phone.
  4. Name it Loyalty Pass, then tap Add or Install.
  5. The merchant can now open Loyalty Pass from the Android Home screen like an app.
Tip

If Android shows Install app, choose it. If it only shows Add to Home screen, that is also correct.
